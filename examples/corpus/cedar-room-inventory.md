# Cedar Room Archive Inventory

The Cedar Room holds field records from the coastal monitoring program. Boxes
are shelved by year and station. Each box has a label with a box number, a year
range, and a station code.

Box C-14 contains Harbor Point calibration records for 2021 and 2022. The 2022
level run is missing from the box; the field book was water damaged and the
values were never transcribed. Box C-15 continues the Harbor Point records for
2023 and 2024 and includes the replacement vent-tube receipt.

Box C-21 holds North Orchard soil-moisture logs from the 2024 irrigation trial.
The logs are printed weekly summaries; the raw fifteen-minute logger files live
on the field laptop, not in the archive. Mira Chen's Friday review notes are in
the same box, clipped to the May and June summaries.

Box C-22 contains survey book HP-2, which records the brass benchmark on the
Harbor Point seawall. The book also includes the 2023 differential level run.
Do not remove HP-2 from the room without logging the loan in the paper register.

The room is dehumidified. A hygrometer on the north wall is read every Monday.
Readings above 60 percent relative humidity should be reported to facilities,
because the older ink on the survey books becomes tacky in damp air.
