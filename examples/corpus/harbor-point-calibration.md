# Tide Gauge Calibration Notes: Harbor Point

Harbor Point station uses a vented pressure sensor, model HG-14, mounted on the
inner face of the east pier. The sensor reports absolute pressure; the logger
subtracts barometric pressure from a second sensor in the instrument hut to
produce water depth.

On 3 March 2023 the station read 42 millimetres below the reference staff. The
technician found a partial blockage in the vent tube, likely salt creep. After
cleaning the tube and replacing the desiccant, the reading returned to within 4
millimetres of the staff.

The station uses a zero offset of 1.284 metres above chart datum. The offset is
checked every six months with a differential level run from the brass benchmark
on the seawall. The benchmark itself is documented in survey book HP-2.

A separate problem appeared in the winter record. Ice formed on the stilling
well and produced short spikes in the series. The spikes were removed by the
quality-control filter, which rejects any sample more than three standard
deviations from a rolling twelve-hour median. The filter is described in the
station metadata file, not in this note.

Calibration records from 2021 through 2024 are stored in the Cedar Room archive.
The 2022 level run is missing; the field book was water damaged and the values
were never transcribed. Do not treat the 2022 offset as verified.
